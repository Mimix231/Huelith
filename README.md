# 🌈 Spectrum
Spectrum is a new free and open-source image editing app that brings alot of new features and eye candy for the image layer to carry!
I am making it so that this app is runnable on both CPU, depending on what type of processor you have, and GPU, GPU has budget settings of max 2GB in the settings overlay, GPU acceleration is supposed to make most effects,filters,styles and fx faster to process when you play with the slider.

Most really nice effects and graphical flair is usually locked behind an subscription, paywall or cancelation fee, And i've always believed that people are allowed to max out their full creativity potentional without having to worry about paying a ridicilous amount of money in order to do it, I mean you can rely on AI to make a edited image for you, But usually that output is full with artifacts that are quite easy to notice, Spectrum should make it easier to make your youtube thumbnail, edited selfie, or whatever way more cooler than it was before.

I also included an Animation Studio so you can make frame by frame animations while also using the effects from the image editor to make them look better, When i first implemented this it was by accident, But then i saw it and decided to keep it.

Well, Have fun using my app!
