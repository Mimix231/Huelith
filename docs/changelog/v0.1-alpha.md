## Known v0.1 Alpha Limitations
- Heavy multi-effect stacks may reduce responsiveness.
- Some effects required further fidelity corrections.
- Automatic background removal struggled with complex character artwork.
- Animation effect persistence still needed a dedicated track-wide architecture.
- Not all effects had native GPU implementations.
