## workspace originals

[Concept sheet](workspace-originals.png)

Use-case ui-mockup. Create one polished theme design board for Huelith, an original desktop video and image editing app. Each labeled theme row is a separate realistic editor mockup at the same scale with a media library left, centered canvas showing a stylized purple dusk mountain landscape with a hiker, properties right and colored multitrack timeline below. Keep editing controls legible, real, compact, and consistent between rows, while theme surfaces, accents, geometry and material details differ. Show actual UI chrome in each row plus a few labeled palette chips in its margin. No marketing taglines, watermarks or logos of existing software. No glow on text, heavy gradients behind small text, subscription badges, or gradients painted over the footage. Typography understated, high-contrast labels. Draw the theme names verbatim. One landscape design sheet.
Five distinct rows: Glass (deep slate and lavender, frosted edge highlights, generous curve); Material (graphite and cool mauve, tactile tonal elevation and lavender pill); Classic (warm pearl-gray light workstation, square inset frames, navy selection); Midnight (midnight blue, luminous periwinkle rule lines, restrained navy bevel); Studio (neutral charcoal, high clarity aqua, tight editor docking). Make the similarities and differences obvious even at thumbnail scale.
