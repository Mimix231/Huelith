# Huelith — All 221 Effects: Intended vs. Current Implementation

**Updated 29 October 2026 — implementation pass, not a completion certificate.**

Baseline: archive `5522b355-f4d2-4222-a73a-3be4007cc85f.zip`. Current: source archive `Huelith_Improvement_Pass_2026-10-29.zip`. 
All **221 catalog entries** remain represented below; the **15 Huelith Originals** are separate and are not part of these 221.

## Read this before interpreting the statuses

- **Should do** describes a reasonable interpretation of the name, not an established acceptance test supplied by its developers.
- **Actually does now** describes inspected source implementation. The baseline description is retained where this pass did not change the algorithm.
- **Changes tested** means targeted browser-level behavior tests passed; it does **not** mean visual fidelity is certified on real artwork, exports, animation, CPU/GPU, or Windows.
- **Unchanged** does not mean broken or complete. Many previously reviewed implementations remain preliminary. The old assessment appears in the final column.
- **No effect is marked “Finished.”** The rendering and editor workflows have not undergone comprehensive per-effect certification.

## What was actually changed

- **25 individual catalog entries** received direct source changes or Studio discovery corrections in this pass. Other operations may be indirectly affected by shared corrections, but they are **not counted as individually completed**.
- Expanded geometry now samples displaced alpha into previously transparent destination pixels, instead of unconditionally clipping to destination alpha.
- Expanded effect schemas now hide many unsupported generic controls; six operations still expose at least one control with no pixel difference in a particular 48×48 reference test (eight control observations). This is a test observation, not proof of universal inactivity.
- Three previously confirmed no-op/near-no-op expansion effects were revised: Manga Screentones, Scratch Ink, and Sparkling Highlights.
- The four pre-existing drawing styles now preserve their source at zero effect amount.
- Background Removal is discoverable in the Studio Effects catalog as well as the inspector.

### Verification (actual checks performed)

- **354/354** Node tests passed after updating two outdated catalog-count assertions for the seventh Layer Effect.
- Chromium confirmed **120/120** expansion outputs altered a representative opaque source at 90% and that **120/120** were distinct on that image. This is **not** evidence that all names are accurately implemented.
- Chromium confirmed 120/120 expanded effects preserved source output at 0 amount, and the four original drawing styles did likewise.
- Dedicated Chromium probes confirmed pixel reordering preserves row pixel multisets at 100%, Film Halation can spread around bright pixels outside their original alpha, and manually positioned Energy Eyes/Lens Ghost respond to center changes.
- Narrow TypeScript compilation covering four edited modules and transitive local imports passed. **Full dependency-aware TypeScript build and Windows/Tauri smoke tests were not completed** (installation of dependencies did not finish in this environment).
- CPU/GPU equivalence, 1080p/4K performance, animation playback, saved project migration, and actual artist-reviewed visual matching for every effect remain open.

### Remaining known architectural problems

- The full catalog still includes many intentionally simplified representations of complex media/optical/lighting techniques. **221/221 are NOT certified complete**.
- The 120 expansion filters are Canvas2D/worker processors, not individual wgpu compute implementations. Native GPU effect coverage is limited.
- Several artistic effects still have no material simulation, image segmentation, true camera model, or professional-quality color pipeline; see row descriptions and initial assessments.
- Changing center/angle/scale may yield no *observable* difference under symmetric settings or sparse highlight input even if the underlying parameter is mathematically consumed.
- Performance improvements from the new highlight sampling and perspective processing require measurements on representative 1080p/4K documents before release.
