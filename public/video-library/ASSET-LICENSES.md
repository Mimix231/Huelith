# Huelith Video Library

The generated artwork in `stickers/huelith-sticker-atlas.png` and the legacy
synthesized assets in `audio/` are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The legacy files
remain available to existing projects that reference them.

The Audio tab searches Wikimedia Commons for recorded audio marked CC0 or
CC BY. Each import carries the original recording page, creator and licence.
Use **Save audio credits** in the export dialog to save credits for the audible
recordings in the sequence.

The human OC preview was generated from the Huelith creator's supplied character
reference. Its processed thumbnails demonstrate Huelith's real renderers. This
license does not claim ownership of the creator's pre-existing character or of
any media that users import. Those retain their original ownership and terms.
